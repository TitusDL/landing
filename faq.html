TitusDL CRM

Straight answers

Questions agents ask before they join.

How do I get my clients in?

Once you’ve signed in and accepted the BAA, upload your client list (Excel, Google Sheets, or an export from your old CRM) on the Import screen. We check it and load it for you at no charge, within 3 days. Please don’t email client files to us.

When do I start paying?

If you join in 2026: it's free until then, and on January 1, 2027 you start at the early-bird price ($59.99 a month for Pro, or $99.99 for Premium if you're one of the 27 early Premium members), kept for all of 2027. We'll announce prices beyond 2027 during 2027. If you join from 2027 on: the regular price from the day you join.

What if I decide not to keep it?

Tell us before January 1, 2027 and you pay nothing. Joining takes no card, so there is nothing to cancel on a payment form. Daily use on your own clients is the fairest test we can offer.

Why is it free until 2027?

We’re building a community of agents, and the agents who join first set its direction. Founding members use it free through 2026 and their requests are built first. The workflow itself has run a working agency’s book for a year and a half.

What happens when the free period ends?

Billing starts at your early-bird price. Your clients, dates, to-dos and alerts stay exactly as they are, and Premium’s daily limits go up.

Are there limits while it’s free?

Pro has none. On Premium, through December 31, 2026, each agent can upload up to 3 applications and record up to 3 voice memos a day. That keeps the free period fair for everyone. Once you’re paying in 2027, the limits go up to fit a full book.

Do you sign a BAA?

Yes. A Business Associate Addendum is part of opening your account, and the Google Cloud services we run on are covered by Google's own BAA.

When can I start?

Now. Join early access and we open your account and send you a short setup guide. Sign in, accept the BAA, upload your client list, and your book is in within 3 days.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

On Google Cloud, in US data centers, under Google Cloud’s own HIPAA agreement, and it’s only accessed from the US. Your book lives in a private database, not a shared spreadsheet. Other agents can’t open your clients, even inside the same agency, and we re-test that before every update. Every login needs a password and a code from your phone, and you’re signed out after 30 minutes idle. TitusDL CRM never emails client information; reminders stay inside the app.

Do I pay sales tax?

It depends on your state. Some states tax software subscriptions and some don't. Where yours does, we add the state and local sales tax for your business address to each bill. California starts on January 1, 2027. Listed prices don't include tax.

Does it work on mobile and tablet?

Yes. It runs in the browser on your phone, tablet or desktop. Add it to your phone's home screen to get push alerts.
